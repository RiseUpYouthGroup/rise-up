Add Rise Up to Your Phone

You can save Rise Up to your Home Screen so it opens like an app.

On iPhone:

1. Open this site in Safari.
2. Tap the Share button.
3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
4. Tap Add.

On Android:

Tap Install below. If the button is unavailable, open the browser menu and choose Add to Home screen or Install app.
